## Long description

**Helm puts you back in control of your Mac.**

Storage fills up quietly — caches pile on, Xcode leaves gigabytes of derived data behind, downloads gather dust, and macOS hides most of it inside an opaque “System Data” blob. Helm cuts through all of it.

The **Storage Manager** scans your whole drive in the background and shows you the truth: an exact capacity reading (purgeable space included, just like *About This Mac*), a clean breakdown by category, and a gorgeous interactive **treemap** you can drill into folder by folder, all the way down to individual files.

When you're ready to reclaim space, Helm's **System Junk cleaner** rounds up caches, logs, the Trash, developer junk, browser data, old downloads and leftover app files — pre-selecting only what's safe and leaving the rest for you to opt into. Find your biggest **large & old files**, hunt down true **duplicates** (matched byte-for-byte), and thin stray **APFS snapshots**.

And because peace of mind matters: **Helm never deletes destructively.** Everything goes to the Trash, recoverable, unless you explicitly choose to empty it.

And Helm is a whole toolkit: alongside Storage it includes **Memory** (free up RAM, quit greedy apps), an **Uninstaller** that removes apps *and* every leftover they hide, **Startup** (tame login items), **Privacy** (clear traces, flush DNS), **Battery** (health & cycle insights), and **Network** (live throughput and connections).

Helm runs **entirely on your Mac** — no servers, no analytics, no tracking. It's **free and open source**.
